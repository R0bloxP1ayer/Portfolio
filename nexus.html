FAQ

Questions answered.

Is my data used to train your models?

No. Your data is never used to train or fine-tune any AI model. All processing happens in isolated, encrypted environments. We are SOC 2 Type II certified.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade whenever you need — changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Downgrades never delete your data.

What happens when I hit my word limit?

On the Starter plan, AI writing pauses until your next billing cycle. You'll get an email with 3 days notice before the limit is reached. You can upgrade at any time to continue immediately.

Do you offer a self-hosted option?

Yes, the Business plan includes an on-premises deployment option with a dedicated Docker image and Helm chart for Kubernetes. Contact our sales team for details.
