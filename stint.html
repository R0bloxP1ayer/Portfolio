    Questions

    Does the timer keep running if I close the tab?

    Yes. The timer runs on our servers, so it keeps counting until you stop it, on any device.

    Can I bill different rates for different clients?

    Every client and project can have its own hourly rate, and you can override it on a single entry.

    Which currencies and tax rules are supported?

    Invoices can be issued in 30 currencies, with configurable tax lines such as VAT or GST.

    Can I export my data?

    Always. Export timesheets and invoices as CSV or PDF from the settings page.

    What happens when I go over the Free plan?

    Nothing breaks. You keep all your data and can upgrade whenever you add a second client.
